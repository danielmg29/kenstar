# Construction (`app/construction/page.tsx`)

## Scope and mode

- **Visitor mode:** Persuade (a service page that leads to a project conversation).
- **Scope:** the construction and roadworks route. Project detail pages are not part of this surface.

## Job, audience and action

- **Who arrives:** builders, renovators and landowners in and around Embu deciding whether Kenstar can take on their structure, civil works or road, and whether its showroom can finish the job.
- **Primary action:** start a project conversation (the project enquiry, sent by WhatsApp or email).
- **Close second:** go to the showroom ranges that finish each stage.
- **Content:** the confirmed services (building works, civil works, beautification and finishing; bitumen, murram and access roads; delivery of materials; equipment and tool hire), Kenstar's four project photographs, the confirmed figures (50+ completed projects, 100+ satisfied clients), and the journey's renders, always labelled as visualisations.
- **Constraints:** construction never leads the brand: the page ends in the showroom as much as in the enquiry. No project names, clients, dates, locations, prices or timelines.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The journey's stages become the service list. Kenstar lays the road in and raises the structure, and its showroom supplies, services and finishes the space: one stage strip from Approach to Finish, each stage naming who does it.

OWN-WORLD: The approved system: a midnight first viewport carrying the journey's structure render full-bleed under a "Visualisation" plate, a midnight copy plate, a stage strip in Manrope with tech-caps stage numbers; the stages below set as ruled limestone sheets with Kenstar's own project photographs as proof and links into the showroom ranges. Brass only for the current stage and the one cut-corner primary action.

STORY: At a glance the visitor sees that Kenstar builds, and that the same business finishes what it builds. They follow the stages from the road to the furnished room, see real project photographs, and start a project conversation. They come away believing one Embu business can carry the job from structure to finish.

## Signature interaction

The stage strip is the page's index: each cell jumps to its stage, and the cell for the stage in view stays lit as the visitor scrolls. Each stage ends in a real action: ask about that service on WhatsApp, or open the showroom range that supplies it. Motion is feedback only.
