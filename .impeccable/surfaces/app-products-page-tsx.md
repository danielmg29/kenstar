# Products (`app/products/page.tsx`)

## Scope and mode

- **Visitor mode:** Persuade (a catalogue that leads to a visit or an enquiry).
- **Scope:** the products and showroom catalogue route. Range detail pages are not part of this surface.

## Job, audience and action

- **Who arrives:** product explorers, homeowners, builders and renovators, mostly on phones, deciding whether the Embu showroom has what they need.
- **Primary action:** add sub-categories to a schedule and send it (WhatsApp or email), or keep it to show at the showroom.
- **Close second:** visit the showroom: today's hours, live open status, Directions.
- **Content:** six ranges; the sub-categories Kenstar publishes (Lighting 6, Electricals 2, Bathroom 5, Furniture 8; Hardware and Finishing have none published, so they are enquiry rows); real June 2026 showroom photos for every published sub-category.
- **Constraints:** no prices, stock, delivery or payment. No product names. Stand-in photos only where labelled Illustrative.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The catalogue is a finishes schedule the visitor fills in: ruled rows, each carrying a real showroom photograph, ticked into "Your schedule", which becomes one WhatsApp or email message or a list to show at the counter. It refuses the shop grid of filters, prices and carts.

OWN-WORLD: The approved system: limestone drawing-sheet ground with graphite hairline rules, midnight title block and the docked schedule as a midnight Field Note plate with its 14px cut, brass only for selection and the one cut-corner primary action. Manrope 600/700 for headings, Inter for text, tech caps for schedule codes (LT-01), which are real references a visitor quotes to staff.

STORY: At a glance the visitor sees that every range in the Embu showroom is listed with real photographs. They scan a section, tick rows into their schedule, then send it or bring it to Market Road. They come away believing the showroom is large, ordered and worth the trip.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A title block in five columns on limestone holds the H1 "Everything a space needs, on one floor in Embu.", the lede, and a ruled meta row: 6 ranges, 200+ products, live open status. A seven-column photograph of the lighting hall carries a caption plate. Beneath sits the sticky schedule index of six range codes. The first section starts above the fold. "Your schedule" docks on the right with its count and Send.

FORM: The finishes schedule, position 1 of 7 on my list; seed:41833ce6fe82073d.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction

Ticking a row stamps it into "Your schedule", with the count changing and the row's mark filling brass. The schedule persists on the device. It sends as one message (WhatsApp or email) listing codes and names, and opens a "show at the showroom" view: a full-screen list for the counter. Motion is feedback only; the site animates only its three experiences.

## Recorded departures (finish review, 2026-11-21)

- **H1:** "Everything a space needs, in one showroom on Market Road." replaces "…on one floor in Embu.": the showroom photographs show a balcony and an upper floor, so "one floor" would be untrue.
- **Home decor:** filed under Furniture & home (FU-09), not Lighting: its photographs show framed pictures and decorative glass for walls and shelves. The counts read Lighting 5 and Furniture 9.
