# About (`app/about/page.tsx`)

## Scope and mode

- **Visitor mode:** Persuade (who Kenstar is, proved by the showroom and four confirmed facts).
- **Scope:** the About route.

## Job, audience and action

- **Who arrives:** first-time visitors checking that Kenstar is real, established and worth the trip or the call.
- **Primary action:** visit the showroom (directions) or explore the products.
- **Close second:** the rest of what Kenstar does (construction, roadworks, delivery and hire, accommodation).
- **Content:** the four facts the user confirmed (200+ products, 100+ satisfied clients, 50+ completed projects, wholesale prices); the six ranges; the services; the showroom address and hours; showroom photographs.
- **Constraints:** no years in business, founders, staff, awards, testimonials or client names. Retail leads; construction never becomes the identity.

## Direction contract

THESIS: About as a title block of facts under the showroom: a large showroom photograph, then the four confirmed figures ruled like a drawing's title block, then one short paragraph and the index of what Kenstar does. It refuses the founder story, the timeline and the team grid it has no facts for.

OWN-WORLD: The approved system on limestone: a wide showroom photograph with a caption plate, a ruled title block with a 2px midnight rule and graphite hairlines, figures in Manrope 600, labels in Inter, tech caps for groups; midnight for the visit band; brass only for the one cut-corner primary action.

STORY: At a glance the visitor sees the showroom and four plain facts. They read what Kenstar is in one paragraph, see everything it does in one ruled index, and get the address and hours. They come away believing Kenstar is a substantial, real business on Market Road.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The furniture floor photograph across the full width with a caption plate. Beneath it the title block: 200+ products in the showroom, 100+ satisfied clients, 50+ completed projects, Wholesale prices. Then the H1 "A hardware and home showroom in Embu." with one paragraph beside it.
