# Accommodation (`app/accommodation/page.tsx`)

## Scope and mode

- **Visitor mode:** Persuade (an interiors feature that leads to an availability enquiry).
- **Scope:** the furnished accommodation route.

## Job, audience and action

- **Who arrives:** visitors and families looking for a furnished place to stay, often referred from the rest of the site.
- **Primary action:** ask about availability (dates, guests, name and phone), sent by WhatsApp or email.
- **Close second:** look through the rooms.
- **Content:** Kenstar's ten photographs of the accommodation (lounge, kitchen, bedroom, details); notes that say only what the photographs show.
- **Constraints:** location, number of units, rates and booking are NOT confirmed: the page says so plainly and never implies a booking engine. No reviews, amenities lists or claims beyond the photographs.

## Signature interaction

The room plates are the index: each opens its room's spread further down (photograph, notes, the other photographs of that room). The enquiry checks the dates make sense and writes one message. Motion is feedback only.
